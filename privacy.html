Privacy

How we handle website enquiries.

A plain-language explanation of the information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 26 July 2026

D’Magical Touch Empire respects your privacy. This notice applies to information submitted through the contact and partnership forms on this website.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, organization, role, enquiry type, subject, message, proposed partnership details, and technical information used to protect the forms from abuse. Investment partnership forms request only an indicative range and do not request bank, card, or identity-document details.

How we use it

We use this information to review and respond to your enquiry, maintain a record of business correspondence, protect the website, and improve how enquiries are managed. Submitting an enquiry does not create a partnership, investment relationship, or contract.

Storage and service providers

Enquiry records are stored using Neon, our database provider. Email notifications may be delivered to our team using Resend. These providers process information only as needed to provide their respective services.

Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as it remains reasonably useful for correspondence, legal obligations, security, or legitimate business recordkeeping. Records that are no longer required may be deleted or anonymized.

Sharing

We do not sell contact-form information. We may share it with authorized team members, service providers, advisers, or authorities when reasonably necessary to respond, operate the website, protect rights, or comply with law.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information you submitted, subject to any recordkeeping or legal obligations that apply. Contact us using the address below.
